## Challenge

Generating a short video with AI models is a chain of paid steps: images, clips, voice, music,
composition. A wrong prompt in the first step is only noticed after the last one has been paid for.

## Solution

Video Lab plans before it spends. A director agent turns an idea into a concrete plan — scenes,
prompts, audio, estimated cost — and nothing is generated until that exact plan is approved.
Changing the plan requires a new approval. Compositions can then be edited scene by scene and
rendered to MP4.

## Architecture

Providers are connected with the user's own keys, which are stored encrypted. Hosted APIs and
self-hosted open models share one interface, and every paid generation is recorded in a cost
ledger with budgets per plan and per day. Without a provider the whole pipeline runs on free
mock media, so the workflow can be developed and tested without cost.
