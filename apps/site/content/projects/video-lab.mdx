## Challenge

Generating a short video with AI models is a chain of paid steps: images, clips, voice, music,
composition. A wrong prompt in the first step is only noticed after the last one has been paid for.

## Solution

Video Lab plans before it spends. A director agent turns an idea into a concrete plan — scenes,
prompts, audio, estimated cost — and nothing is generated until that exact plan is approved.
Changing the plan requires a new approval. Compositions can then be edited scene by scene and
rendered to MP4. The application is local-first and is not offered as a hosted service; this page
shows the project and its synthetic storyboard, not a public product demo.

## Architecture

Providers — OpenAI, Google, fal.ai, ElevenLabs and others — are connected with the user's own
keys, which are stored encrypted with the master key in the operating system's keychain.
Self-hosted open models run on Modal behind a gateway. Every paid generation is recorded in a
cost ledger, with a cost estimate per plan and budget limits. Without a provider the whole pipeline runs on free
mock media, so the workflow can be developed and tested without cost.
